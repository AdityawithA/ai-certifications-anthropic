## What the Course Is About

This course adapts Anthropic's core AI Fluency Framework for people running or working in small businesses — customer-facing staff, back-office management, supply chain operations, and leadership. It was built in partnership with real small business operators (PayPal, a butcher shop, and a rebuilders enterprise), grounding it in research on what small business professionals actually need, worry about, and hope for with AI adoption — rather than generic corporate AI training.

## Key Concepts

### 1. The 4 Ds, Applied to Small Business Work
- **Delegation** — deciding which tasks (customer replies, inventory tracking, scheduling, marketing copy) make sense to hand to AI given limited staff and no dedicated tech team
- **Description** — clearly directing AI so its output matches your business's voice, policies, and customer expectations
- **Discernment** — checking AI output before it reaches customers, suppliers, or financial records — since a small business has little room to absorb costly mistakes
- **Diligence** — using AI responsibly and being transparent with customers and staff about where it's involved

### 2. Small Business-Specific Considerations
- **Limited resources** — no in-house IT or AI specialists, so tools need to be practical and easy to adopt without heavy setup
- **Direct customer relationships** — small businesses often run on trust and personal relationships; AI missteps (wrong info, generic tone) can damage that faster than at a large company
- **Cross-functional roles** — the same person may handle customer service, ops, and marketing, so the course covers AI use across multiple day-to-day functions rather than one department
- **Real-world grounding** — examples drawn from actual small business use cases (retail, food service, operations/logistics)

### 3. Practical Application Areas
- Customer interactions (responses, FAQs, order support)
- Back-office management (scheduling, bookkeeping support, admin)
- Supply chain and inventory operations
- Marketing and communications on a limited budget

## What I Learned

- Small businesses get outsized value from AI precisely *because* of resource constraints — automating repetitive admin and customer-facing drafts frees up time that would otherwise go to a role the business can't afford to hire for.
- The stakes for Discernment are high in a small business: there's often no second layer of review before something reaches a customer, so catching AI errors before they go out matters more than in a large org with more checks.
- Voice and tone consistency matters a lot for small businesses that run on personal trust — generic AI output can undercut the very thing that differentiates them from bigger competitors.
- The framework holds up the same way it does elsewhere (Delegation → Description → Discernment → Diligence), but the risk profile and available resources change *how* each competency gets applied.

## One-Line Takeaway
> For small businesses, AI fluency means using limited AI tooling to punch above your resource constraints — without ever letting it erode the personal trust the business is built on.
